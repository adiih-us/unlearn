03 / Guidelines

Rules & Qualification

Simple guidelines to keep the sprint fair, collaborative, and fun for all freshers.

🎓

1st-Year Eligibility

Exclusive to first-year students of any engineering, science, or degree college. No prior coding experience needed.

🎯

3,000 Karma Target

Participants must accumulate at least 3,000 verifiable Karma points on μJourney between 16 and 27 August to qualify for swag.

🤝

Squad Collaboration

Work closely with your assigned 5-peer squad. Teamwork and peer learning are key pillars of the μLearn philosophy.

🛡️

Genuine Proof of Work

Zero tolerance for plagiarism. Submissions are peer-reviewed. Code must be your own or properly attributed.

💬

Daily Mentor Syncs

Attend daily quick voice/text syncs on Discord with your assigned volunteer mentor to clear roadblocks.
